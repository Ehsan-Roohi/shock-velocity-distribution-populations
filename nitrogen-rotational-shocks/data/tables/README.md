# Tables of the Supplemental Material as CSV

Each file holds one table of the Supplemental Material, in its order there (Table S1, S2, ...), with the header rows as printed. Captions:

- `table_S1_traj.csv`: Single collision of a cold stream molecule with a molecule that moves upstream: classical trajectories. Columns are defined in the text.
- `table_S2_elo.csv`: The same for other elongations of the molecule (bond length over site diameter); nitrogen is 0.345.
- `table_S3_energy.csv`: Single collision of a stream molecule in bins of the relative translational energy E_t before the collision; columns are defined in the text.
- `table_S4_mixture.csv`: Distribution after the first collision of a stream molecule in the shock (trajectory kernel): local slope -1/(dln P/dE), in units of T_2, of the DSMC distribution, of the sum of the trajectory distributions over the collision energies of the first collisions, and of the same sum with Eq. (1) of the Letter extended to all shares; mean pair energy < E_tot> of the collisions that leave the molecule above E, and the share this corresponds to.
- `table_S5_sharescale.csv`: Local scale of the single-collision distribution in the share x of the pair energy, -1/(dlnsigma/dx) between the shares given, in bins of E_t (trajectories, conditions of the two shocks).
- `table_S6_kernel.csv`: Single collision through the kernel and from the direct trajectories: cross-section sigma^g_x for a gain of rotational energy above x kT_2 in units of the VHS cross-section, and temperature of the exponential through the values at 1 and 3 kT_2.
- `table_S7_numerics.csv`: Shock simulations with the trajectory kernel and changed numerical parameters (two runs each, the reference four; Boltzmann stream at T_1, unscattered population replaced by the measured one). Level populations at the stations of Table 'levels': root-mean-square deviation of predicted over measured population from one, its range, and root-mean-square change of the predicted populations against the reference runs. Hot group: root-mean-square difference between the share of molecules with three or more collisions and the measured hot group over all stations, as in Fig. 3 of the Letter. T_s: temperature of the exponential fitted between 1 and 3 kT_2 to the rotational energy after the first collision. L^*/delta: inverse density thickness (measured 0.158 and 0.167). Delta: distance between the density and rotational-temperature profiles (measured 1.9 and 2.54).
- `table_S8_levels.csv`: Residual (in per cent) of the level populations predicted by DSMC, with no fitted number, against the measured ones ; last column: range of predicted over measured population at the tabulated levels for the trajectory kernel. The last line of each block is the root-mean-square deviation of that ratio from one over all tabulated levels.
- `table_S9_expfit.csv`: Least-squares fits of the exponential tails: first collisions in the shock, and the molecules with one, two, and three or more collisions at three stations (trajectory kernel). Fitted temperature, root-mean-square and largest deviation of ln P(e_rot>E) from the fit, and range of the local slope over intervals of 0.5 kT_2.
- `table_S10_first.csv`: First counted collision of a stream molecule in the shock: mean relative translational energy before it, mean rotational energy after it, probabilities of rotational energy above 0.3, 1 and 3 kT_2 after it, and the exponential through the values at 1 and 3 kT_2. Trajectory kernel (deflection threshold 26 deg, and 37 deg and 14 deg in two runs each) and LB rule with p(E_c).
- `table_S11_counts.csv`: Molecules by number of collisions at the measured stations (trajectory kernel, deflection threshold 26 deg): shares, temperatures T_s of the groups with one, two, and three or more collisions, share of the molecules on the measured hot line that have three or more collisions, and the hot-group fraction from the measurement .
- `table_S12_threshold.csv`: Dependence on the deflection threshold that counts a collision in the kernel runs (two runs each for 14 deg and 37 deg): shares of molecules with three or more collisions at the stations of Table 'counts', range of the temperatures of the groups over the stations, and root-mean-square difference between the share with three or more collisions and the measured hot-group fraction at these stations.
- `table_S13_profiles.csv`: Profiles and upstream-moving molecules for the four exchange descriptions; symbols in the text.
- `table_S14_precursor.csv`: Decay of the upstream-moving fraction ahead of the shock: simulations and linear cascade. The runs at Mach 8.0 and 12.0 have the Boltzmann stream at T_1.
